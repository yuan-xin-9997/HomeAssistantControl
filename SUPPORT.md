# Support

- Store, activation and refunds: <https://hacontrol.yuan-xin.top/>
- Email: <support@yuan-xin.top>
- Compatibility notes: <https://hacontrol.yuan-xin.top/compatibility>
- Setup guide: <https://hacontrol.yuan-xin.top/guide>

When contacting support, include:

- your order number from the Creem receipt;
- the app version shown in the sidebar;
- your operating system;
- the device model and firmware version, if the problem is device-specific;
- the exact error message, with any credentials or tokens removed.

Never send your Xiaomi password, SunLogin password, device token, private key or full activation code in a public issue.
